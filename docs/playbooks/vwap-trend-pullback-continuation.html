    VWAP Trend-Pullback Continuation Research Template

    untested-research-hypothesis
    Untested research hypothesis. Not financial advice, a recommendation, or evidence of profitability.

    Market: generic-crypto-perpetual-futures · Signal: 15m · Context: 1h, 4h

    Hypothesis

    During an established higher-timeframe trend, a controlled 15-minute pullback toward VWAP or anchored VWAP may resume in the trend direction after rejection, net of costs.

    Regime profile

    Favored research context: trend.uptrendtrend.downtrendvolatility.normalliquidity.normal

    Avoid: trend.rangetrend.transitionvolatility.shockliquidity.dislocatedpositioning.deleveraging

    Entry conditions

    Long

    • Both context timeframes meet the frozen uptrend definition.
    • Price pulls back to the tested VWAP zone without breaking the structural invalidation level.
    • A closed 15m bar rejects the zone and resumes above it.
    • Liquidity and after-cost reward-to-risk meet caller-supplied constraints.

    Short

    • Both context timeframes meet the frozen downtrend definition.
    • Price rallies to the tested VWAP zone without breaking the structural invalidation level.
    • A closed 15m bar rejects the zone and resumes below it.
    • Liquidity and after-cost reward-to-risk meet caller-supplied constraints.

    Invalidation

    • The higher-timeframe trend definition no longer holds.
    • Price closes beyond the predeclared swing or anchored-VWAP invalidation level.
    • The market enters volatility.shock, liquidity.dislocated, or positioning.deleveraging.

    Exit logic

    • Use a predeclared volatility or structural stop and simulate gap-through execution.
    • Test a trailing exit based on favorable extreme minus/plus the configured ATR distance.
    • Apply a time exit if continuation does not develop within the configured holding window.
    • Include funding cash flows at exact payment timestamps.

    Cost model

    Model passive and aggressive entry variants separately and prevent favorable same-bar fills when bar ordering is unknown.

    • maker-taker-fees
    • spread
    • slippage
    • market-impact
    • funding

    Risk constraints

    • Derive quantity from a caller-supplied risk budget and the wider of structural and volatility stop distance.
    • Cap by executable depth, leverage ceiling, and liquidation-distance buffer.
    • Reject entries whose estimated cost removes the configured minimum after-cost payoff.

    Failure modes

    • The trend classification lags a reversal and labels transition as continuation.
    • The VWAP anchor is selected with hindsight.
    • Funding or crowding makes continuation vulnerable to a squeeze.
    • Same-bar execution assumptions create impossible entries and stops.

    Validation plan

    • Define trend and anchors before evaluation and retain every rejected signal in the audit log.
    • Walk forward chronologically across multiple venues and both directions.
    • Run sensitivity analysis across adjacent parameters rather than report only the best combination.
    • Report after-cost expectancy, drawdown, tail loss, fill rate, and regime-conditioned stability on untouched data.
